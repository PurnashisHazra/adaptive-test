Customer journey roadmap

A simple view of how admins and students move through the AdapTest workflow.

  1. Step 1

    Admin adds or generates questions

    Faculty can manually add questions, or use AI-assisted generation to build quality question banks faster.

  2. Step 2

    Student takes an adaptive test

    Difficulty automatically adjusts with each response so every student gets a path aligned to current performance.

  3. Step 4

    Student reviews detailed analytics

    Students and mentors get breakdowns by topic, speed, accuracy, and attempt behavior to understand performance.

  4. Step 5

    Student gets score-maximizing strategy

    The platform turns insights into exact actions so learners know what to practice, what to skip, and how to maximize score.
